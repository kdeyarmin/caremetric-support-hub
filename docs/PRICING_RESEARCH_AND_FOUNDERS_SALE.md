# CareMetric pricing review and Founders Sale

Researched and verified November 8, 2026. All prices are USD, before applicable taxes. PennTrain is the repository name for CareBase and CareMetric Train; Intel means CareMetric Intel.

## Recommendation

Keep the current regular prices for this launch. Use **per-facility subscription pricing for CareBase**, **flat organization pricing for Train**, and **organization tiers with included seats, branches and territories for Intel**. Preserve monthly and annual options, and introduce **50% off the first 12 months of paid service** through the Founders Sale.

These are the best-supported launch models from the available evidence, not a measured profit-maximizing price. Customer willingness to pay, acquisition cost, churn and delivery costs were not available. Validate those before making a permanent price cut or increasing the upper tiers.

The sale is configured in the live CareMetric Stripe account as **FOUNDERS50**. The regular catalog prices were read from Stripe and agree with the existing product fact sheet. The live public sites corroborate CareBase's monthly prices and Intel's displayed annual prices. No existing customer subscription was changed.

## Recommended packaging

### CareBase: retain per-facility pricing

Keep Core at **$199/month per active facility**, Small Home at **$199** including eMAR for facilities with **fewer than 20 licensed beds**, Compliance Suite at **$279**, Small Home Complete at **$399**, and Complete at **$649**. Residents and staff stay unlimited. Do not convert to per-resident pricing: facility pricing is understandable, predictable, and avoids charging customers more for entering complete records.

Lead the buying page with three choices: **Core**, **Complete**, and **Training only**. Put Small Home eligibility and Compliance Suite alongside the relevant use cases rather than presenting every SKU as a competing primary plan. Keep modules available for customers who already have an EHR or eMAR.

At current monthly prices, Core plus all five add-ons totals **$794**; Complete at **$649** saves **$145/month** (about **18.3%**) against buying those same CareBase items separately. Compliance Suite at **$279** saves **$68/month** against Training + Workforce + Survey & Compliance at **$347**. These are internal bundle comparisons, not competitor savings claims.

General Core does not include eMAR: Core + eMAR is **$348/month**, or **$174/month** in the sale. The under-20-bed Small Home plan includes eMAR for **$199/month**, or **$99.50/month** in the sale. Keep that distinction prominent.

### Train: flat pricing with a low-cost single-facility entry

Keep **$119/month for one facility** and **$239/month per organization**, with unlimited learners. This provides an affordable entry for small homes and avoids a per-learner penalty for training every employee.

Do not imply an identical curriculum or accreditation to CareAcademy or Relias. For very large rollouts, measure support, content-production and video costs; negotiate implementation services separately rather than silently narrowing existing unlimited-learner promises.

### Intel: platform tiers with included capacity

Keep **Starter $349**, **Growth $899**, **Pro $1,999 per organization per month**, with Enterprise quoted separately. Retain the actual limits:

| Plan | Included team | Branches | Territory |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 3 marketers + 1 manager | 1 | Up to 10 counties |
| Growth | 10 seats | Up to 3 | 1 full state |
| Pro | 25 seats | Up to 10 | Up to 3 states |

Starter's four included seats work out to **$87.25/seat/month** at full utilization; Growth is **$89.90** and Pro **$79.96**. These are explanatory equivalents, not a per-seat billing model or proof of competitor savings.

Sell the predictable starting total and included coverage. Expansion seats, branches and states should be explicit; avoid charging per referral, because referrals are customer-recorded outcomes, not a stable software consumption unit. Avoid pure usage pricing for basic research: customers need predictable budgets and confidence to explore.

Keep existing annual prices (**$3,480 / $8,988 / $19,980**). Keep the existing Growth onboarding waiver for annual billing. Evaluate whether Pro's mandatory **$1,500** onboarding is justified by delivered services, but do not silently waive it or discount it as part of this subscription sale. Growth monthly onboarding remains **$500**. Existing extra-seat prices (**$59 / $49 / $39**), branch (**$99**) and state (**$199**) prices remain unchanged and are excluded from this sale: no active, separately priced Intel expansion products appeared in the inspected catalog.

Do not raise Intel prices merely because enterprise data vendors use quote-based sales. Intel should position its transparent pricing, focused territories and usable CRM workflows without implying it supplies the same proprietary claims data.

### Applying the offer

1. In Hub Billing, sync the catalog from Stripe. Confirm the verified coupon and code appear and check the new paid subscription's product and prior billing history.
2. Give the customer the regular price, discounted price, excluded fees, chosen cadence and renewal terms before activation. Confirm there is no other discount at customer, subscription or item level; do not combine FOUNDERS50 with INTELFOUNDING or another coupon.
3. Start the discount at the beginning of paid service, with the billing cycle aligned to that date. Do not attach the repeating coupon at free-trial signup: its clock starts when applied, not after the free trial. A subscription still in trial needs an aligned end-of-trial activation, not early redemption.
4. Use the Hub's subscription discount controls for a trial conversion, or select the promotion when creating an eligible new paid subscription. Do not create a second subscription if the account already has a trial/subscription; reconcile the existing subscription first. Do not attach the coupon at the whole-customer level.
5. Inspect the actual preview/invoice, discount start/end and renewal price before requesting payment. The exact-amount customer Checkout flow cannot be discounted out of band.
6. Keep the initial monthly/annual cadence and renewal anchor for the introductory year. Eligible plan changes retain the original discount end; do not reapply the coupon or grant a new year. Review annual prorations and cadence changes manually: a repeating coupon discounts invoices generated inside its window, and a new full annual invoice inside that window could extend discounted service beyond the intended year. Schedule cadence changes for the first standard-price renewal.
7. At the first standard-price renewal, the temporary discount expires. Cancellation and restart do not create another eligible first year. Provide renewal reminders through the existing customer communications process.

The old Intel 25%-forever coupons/code and CareBase's legacy founding-partner terms were not modified. Honor previously agreed arrangements. Use the clearly named Founders Sale for new offers; do not advertise it as lifetime pricing.

Stripe's [coupon documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/coupons) explains duration, trial-related first-time restrictions, product scopes and stackable discounts. No automatic tax setting was changed. Prices exclude tax; use the existing tax treatment and review [Stripe Tax for subscriptions](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/taxes/collect-taxes) with applicable registrations before enabling collection.

## Validation and follow-up

Read-after-write verification confirmed the live coupon's exact product allowlist, 50% discount, 12-month duration and active code. No customer was charged or enrolled. A full paid customer conversion and twelve-month renewal simulation were not performed in this task; the assisted process above includes invoice and expiry review before use.

Track trial-to-paid conversion, activation by workflow, support hours, AI/video costs, collected recurring revenue net of discounts, retention and discount-expiry renewals separately by product and billing cadence. Review after the initial cohorts activate and again before year-two renewals. A practical planning target is at least **70% contribution margin after direct service costs**; it is a proposed internal target, not measured performance. At 50% of list price, that leaves a cost budget of 15% of regular revenue: **$29.85/month per Core facility** or **$134.85/month per Intel Growth organization** on monthly billing. Annual sale plans have a lower monthly revenue equivalent and need a tighter budget.

The evidence supports the current pricing structure and a temporary acquisition offer. It does not establish price elasticity or justify claims of guaranteed customer savings, referrals, revenue, or survey outcomes.
